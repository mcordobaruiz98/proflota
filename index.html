CALCULADORA DE FLETES El proyecto trata de una calculadora de fletes. Donde insertemos datos variables de viajes realizados en tractomulas y obtengamos como resultados costos/km, ganancias/ton, margen de viaje y ganancia por viaje. Objetivos, que nuestra calculadora nos diga cuales son los fletes mas rentables y podamos guardar los viajas hasta el punto en que podamos manejar los datos mensuales de nuestro vehiculo con ella. PWA icons (192/512px) — pending designer. --- Key learnings & principles Route cost model: Fixed costs (tolls, fuel, loading/unloading, driver %) are stable per route and should be pre-filled; only freight rate and tonnage change trip-to-trip. The calculator itself serves as the "template" — no separate template abstraction needed. Freight rates are never auto-filled from company records: Rates vary even with the same company, so pre-filling them would introduce errors. DOM rendering discipline: Full re-renders on every keystroke cause focus loss. Solution: separate text input handlers (onStr) from numeric handlers (onNum); only numeric/calculation-affecting inputs trigger re-renders. Data architecture: Vehicle-level data (profile, maintenance, documents) is separated from trip-level data (route economics, cargo, financials). --- Approach & patterns Mario communicates concisely and iteratively, preferring direct and objective responses.
